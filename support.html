When reporting an issue

Include your device model, iOS version, the activity you were using, and the steps that led to the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove any personal information before sending them. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

Restore a subscription

Open HQuize's Settings, choose HQuize subscription, and tap Restore purchases while using the Apple Account that purchased the subscription. An internet connection is required.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open your device's Settings, tap your Apple Account name, then Subscriptions. Select HQuize to view the renewal details or cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refund requests

Apple handles App Store refunds. Visit Apple's Report a Problem page to request a refund.

Saved progress

Progress is stored locally on your device. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting unless you intend to remove that local data. We cannot remotely retrieve your saved progress.
