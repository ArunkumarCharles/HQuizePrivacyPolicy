Privacy Policy

This policy explains how HQuize, an iPhone and iPad learning and games app developed by Arunkumar, handles information. It also explains what happens when you contact us or visit this policy website.

Your learning stays on your device. HQuize does not require an account and does not send your quiz answers, game results, interests, or learning progress to a developer-operated server. The app contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

1. Information stored on your device

HQuize stores information locally to provide its features, including:

  • Quiz answers, bookmarks, review schedules, subject mastery, interests, and unfinished sessions.
  • Game scores, personal bests, saved boards, brain-exercise sessions, and Circuit Lab progress.
  • Daily goals, streaks, achievements, the learning timezone, and appearance, sound, and haptic preferences.

This information is used for saving and restoring sessions, personalizing practice, and showing progress. It is not uploaded by HQuize to us. If saved progress cannot be read, the app may preserve a local recovery copy before writing new progress.

2. App Store purchases and subscriptions

HQuize is free to download and offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Purchases, billing, refunds, and restoration are handled by Apple and require an internet connection.

HQuize uses verified StoreKit purchase information, such as the product identifier, subscription validity, expiration, and revocation status, to determine access to subscription features. This verification happens using Apple's services; HQuize does not send transaction information to a developer-operated backend. We do not receive your payment card details from the app.

Apple handles Apple Account information, payment information, and purchase records under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple may make sales reports, aggregate App Store statistics, or diagnostics available to developers through its services and your device's sharing settings. HQuize does not add its own analytics or diagnostic-upload service.

3. Information you send when contacting us

If you email or call us, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your email address or phone number, message, and any attachments. We use it to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, and maintain necessary support records. Please avoid sending payment details, passwords, or unnecessary personal information. Email and phone providers process communications under their own policies.

4. Sharing and tracking

We do not sell or rent your personal information. HQuize does not use advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, advertising networks, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not receive the local learning and game data listed above.

Support information may be processed by the communication services used to handle your request. We may disclose information we hold if required by applicable law or to protect legal rights. This does not give us access to information stored only on your device.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on your device while the app is installed. Deleting HQuize, rather than offloading it, removes the app's local data from that device. Device or system backups may retain copies under your Apple settings and Apple's policies; restoring a backup may restore app data. HQuize does not provide its own cloud sync service.

Support communications are retained for as long as needed to handle your request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of support information we hold. We cannot remotely inspect or delete data stored only on your device.

Deleting the app does not cancel an App Store subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Apple retains purchase records according to its policies.

6. Children and younger users

HQuize is intended for teenagers aged 13 and older and adults, and is not designed specifically for children under 13. The app does not ask users for a name, email address, date of birth, or account registration. A parent or guardian should supervise purchases and contact us if they believe a child has sent personal information through a support request.

7. Security and your choices

HQuize uses local app storage and Apple's purchase-verification mechanisms. Protect your device with its available security settings. No storage or communication method can guarantee absolute security.

You can change app preferences in Settings, manage Apple purchase and diagnostic-sharing settings, delete the app's local data by deleting the app, or contact us about support information. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. Contact us to make such a request.

8. This website and external links

This policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, or forms. Visiting them involves sending normal web-request information to GitHub, such as your IP address and browser information. GitHub processes hosting information under GitHub's Privacy Statement. Following links to Apple, GitHub, email, or phone services is subject to those services' policies.

9. Changes to this policy

If HQuize's information practices change, we will update this page and its last-updated date. Any new data collection or permissions will be described before being introduced where required.
